
# Practical Intelligence Lab

**Building practical solutions through research, experimentation and technology.**

Practical Intelligence Lab is an independent, open-source initiative focused on identifying real-world problems and developing accessible technological solutions.

## Our Mission

To make useful technology, research and problem-solving accessible to individuals, organisations and communities.

## Areas of Exploration

- Data Science and Analytics
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Software Engineering and Automation
- Financial Engineering
- Scientific Computing and Computational Research

## Our Approach

1. Identify meaningful problems.
2. Investigate and understand them.
3. Develop practical solutions.
4. Test and evaluate our work.
5. Share knowledge and reusable tools.
6. Encourage collaboration and community contributions.

## Current Project

**Small Business Intelligence Toolkit**

An open-source project exploring how data analysis and machine learning can help small businesses understand customer enquiries and operational challenges.

## Philosophy

We believe in open knowledge, accessible technology, responsible experimentation and building things that provide real value.

## Get Involved

This initiative is being developed in public. Contributions, ideas, feedback and collaboration are welcome.
